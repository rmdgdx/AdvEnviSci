Advanced Environmental Science

Six chapters across twelve weeks, each chapter running two full sessions — from the scope and systems logic of the field, through earth systems and spatial analysis, biogeochemistry, and atmospheric chemistry, to impact assessment, risk, and the dynamics that decide whether an ecosystem recovers or flips. Every chapter is a standalone HTML page with figures, worked calculations, a problem set, a self-assessment, and a graded case with model answers.

How the term is built

Each chapter occupies two weeks: the first session establishes the framework, the second puts it to work on data, a model, or a real site. The sequence moves from describing the system, to measuring it, to deciding what to do about it.

Chapters 1–2 · Weeks 1–4Framing and spatial literacy

What the field claims to know and how it knows it, the coupled earth system, and the GIS and remote-sensing skills every later chapter assumes.

Chapters 3–4 · Weeks 5–8Process and chemistry

How matter and energy actually move — element cycles, ecosystem metabolism, and the reactive chemistry of the atmosphere.

Chapters 5–6 · Weeks 9–12Assessment and response

Predicting impact, quantifying risk, and reading the thresholds and resilience that govern whether a system absorbs a disturbance.
